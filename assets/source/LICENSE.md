# OathFront asset provenance and use

The raster illustrations in `art/` are original outputs requested for OathFront through OpenAI's built-in `image_gen` tool. Each selected PNG and its exact prompt are retained here. The asset manifest records hashes and provenance. They do not incorporate downloaded third-party artwork, logos, or licensed characters. Raw AI outputs are not represented as exclusively copyrightable.

The project owner may use, modify, reproduce and distribute these project-generated assets with OathFront, subject to applicable law and the terms governing the generation service. This statement is a provenance/use record, not a claim of exclusive rights or a third-party stock license.

The PCM audio compositions, sound-design source, SVG VFX geometry, and deterministic export scripts were authored specifically for this project. They contain no downloaded audio samples, speech or third-party music. The project owner may use, modify and distribute them with the game. Toolchain dependencies (Python, Pillow and ffmpeg) are not incorporated into the artwork/audio license.

Rules, names, costs and stats are rendered as semantic UI text rather than burned into bitmaps. Missing assets intentionally retain Vietnamese fallback names. Source hashes and export hashes are verified by `tools/assets/validate.ts`.
