# OathFront asset provenance and use

The raster illustrations in `art/`, `rivals-v2/`, and `fivefold-cards-v3/` are original outputs requested for OathFront through OpenAI's built-in `image_gen` tool. Each selected PNG and its exact prompt are retained here. The corresponding source manifests and generation records document hashes and provenance. They do not incorporate downloaded third-party artwork, logos, or licensed characters. Raw AI outputs are not represented as exclusively copyrightable.

The project owner may use, modify, reproduce and distribute these project-generated assets with OathFront, subject to applicable law and the terms governing the generation service. This statement is a provenance/use record, not a claim of exclusive rights or a third-party stock license.

The PCM audio compositions, sound-design source, SVG VFX geometry, and deterministic export scripts were authored specifically for this project. They contain no downloaded audio samples, speech or third-party music. The project owner may use, modify and distribute them with the game. Toolchain dependencies (Python, Pillow and ffmpeg) are not incorporated into the artwork/audio license.

Rules, names, costs and stats are rendered as semantic UI text rather than burned into bitmaps. Current presentation fallbacks use English names; immutable historical core records retain their original Vietnamese labels. Core source hashes and export hashes are verified by `tools/assets/validate.ts`; versioned presentation exports have their own manifests and checks.
