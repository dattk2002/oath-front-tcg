Dawnward

Solenne

The Sunforged Envoy

Protective duelist

Solenne's powers

BASTION / PRISM · 20 HP

  • Dawn Shield
    Give an allied unit Shield. Draw a card.
  • Mirrorstep
    Move an allied unit to an empty allied lane. Give it +1 ATK this turn.
  • Silent Flash
    Stun an enemy unit this turn. Draw a card.
  • Solar Accord · Signature · 1 energy
    Give an allied unit Shield and +1/+1 permanently.

Play pattern: Protect a valuable unit, then move it away from unfavorable exchanges.

Counterplay: Hard removal and wide pressure punish investment in one unit.

Ashen Court

Rook

The Cinder Marksman

Tactical displacement

Rook's powers

FURNACE / VEIL · 20 HP

  • Ember
    Deal 2 damage to an enemy unit or hero.
  • Temper Blade
    Give an allied unit +2 ATK this turn and Shield.
  • False Path
    Move an enemy unit to an empty lane on its side.
  • Crossfire Oath · Signature · 1 energy
    Move an enemy unit to an empty lane on its side. Deal 2 damage to it.

Play pattern: Move a blocker out of the lane you want to attack, then finish a weakened target.

Counterplay: A full enemy board denies movement; Armor and Shield reduce the follow-up damage.
