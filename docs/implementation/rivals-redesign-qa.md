# Rivals presentation: state and QA contract

This is the current implementation contract for the English OathFront interface. The approved [redesign spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-oathfront-rivals-redesign.md), [English ID catalog](../PRESENTATION-EN.md) and [release checklist](release-checklist.md) remain the source for scope and release holds. Historical Vietnamese source/parity tables and the original user reference Markdown remain intact.

## Tokens and typography

`apps/web/src/styles/tokens.css` owns the semantic variables. Dark canvas `--bg #090b10`, surface `--surface #151820`, raised surface `--raised #222733`, light `--text #f8f8f2` and `--muted #bcc2ce` carry the main UI. The paper stage uses `--paper #f0efe8` and `--ink #101218`; `--primary #ffe34d` is the yellow action/focus accent with ink text. Dawnward uses `--good #80e3e0` on dark surfaces and `--good-ink #165f62` on paper; Ashen Court uses `--evil #ff957b` and `--evil-ink #8b392b` respectively. `--error #ffb4ab` pairs text with an icon/message, and `--border #7d8798` marks meaningful boundaries. The seven `--fx-*` variables keep battle effects aligned to the factions without making color the sole status cue. Barlow Condensed 700/800/900 is the display face; Noto Sans remains the body, rules and Unicode fallback. Normal text needs 4.5:1 contrast, large text 3:1 and meaningful control boundaries 3:1. The tested solid pairs are listed in the spec; gradients, overlays and disabled states still need rendered inspection.

## Interaction and state matrix

No hidden power is disclosed before confirmation. |
| Battle/card | Five lanes stay left-to-right; semantic DOM controls mirror decorative Pixi content. Hand-card focus and hover share a reversible 120ms lift/tilt; touch selects then acts through the same legal-target path. Keyboard can inspect/select/activate without drag. | Legal/disabled reasons, cost/name/stats and full inspect rules remain text. Pause, offline and snapshot failure keep the board while disabling unsafe commands; explicit retry/renewal uses the retained command. | Dialog/result/history/settings/auth | Native dialogs place focus on their heading or first action and restore focus when closed. Escape in Pause submits `RESUME` when allowed, cancels its surrender/skip confirmation step first, and does not resume while disabled. Result prevents Escape dismissal and uses explicit exit actions. Other dialogs preserve their own cancel/close behavior. Result, block and pause keep complete actions. History rows and settings use readable text labels and semantic controls. | Entrances do not delay native dismissal. Empty, loading and error states retain meaningful dimensions and next actions. Long account/deck names wrap without translation; a failed provider/logout path retains explicit recovery. |

Interactive targets are at least 44×44 CSS pixels with 8px between adjacent controls. Hover never reveals essential rules or legal targets. Focus outlines remain outside angled decoration. A card collection item has one primary destination; its quantity or retry controls are independent and do not activate the item. No interactive element is nested inside another link/button. The canvas is decorative where the DOM supplies equivalent names and actions. Live regions announce important batches, not every particle frame.

## Motion and interruption

GSAP owns home reveal (480ms), selected hero art/name (300ms with 25ms power stagger), panel/dialog entrance (240ms), phase label (180ms) and hand-card feedback (120ms). A selected hero image decodes before its reveal; its name and radio remain available immediately. Fine-pointer parallax stops on hidden/reduced motion. OS or in-game reduction suppresses travel, zoom and jitter while preserving static effects and every command path. Each owner reverts on unmount/interruption. Battle damage uses the existing authoritative 40% commit beat; EVIL anticipation precedes GOOD by 80ms, signature lasts 550ms, and the seven kinetic/shield/growth/prism/ember/ink/veil families use bounded, reusable geometry. Hidden tabs, pause and reconnect fast-forward cosmetic work without changing authoritative events or receipt semantics.

## Layout and testable QA

At 375px, home, roster, collection, settings and battle fallback must remain readable with no page-wide horizontal scroll. At 1024×640, the five-lane board, hand and End phase must fit; decorative hand/inspect art may collapse. All ten hand cards must retain complete ATK/HP or trick-summary text within their bounds and the viewport; long names and full rules remain available through selection. At 1440×900, inspect, hand art, names/stats and board remain legible. At 200% browser zoom, reflow must leave actions, focus and full rules reachable without clipping. Check light-paper and dark-surface contrast, 44px targets and 8px gaps, first/selected/disabled/pending/error/retry states, keyboard tab/arrows/Enter/Escape, pointer and touch-equivalent selection, normal and reduced motion, rapid interruption, reload/reconnect, missing art and console/page errors.

The production browser suite checks the automated paths; its actual command, counts, failure/covering rerun and screenshots belong in the [redesign evidence index](evidence/rivals-redesign/README.md). A Chromium viewport or zoom emulation does not certify physical touch, Safari/Edge, assistive technology, audio perception or frame p95. The release checklist keeps those and balance/human playtests open.
