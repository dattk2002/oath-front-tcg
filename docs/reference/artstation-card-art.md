# OathFront card-art reference notes

The user explicitly requested ArtStation reference for character/card illustration, approved redrawing all 60 cards, then named the [Marvel Rivals Team-Up roster](https://www.marvelrivals.com/heroes/teamup.html) as the primary style reference for creep cards. Inspected on 2026-10-21. These references guide composition and production decisions; their artwork is not copied into the game or supplied as an image-generation input.

## Observed sources

- [Marvel Rivals Team-Up roster](https://www.marvelrivals.com/heroes/teamup.html): browser observation shows angular stylized character planes, strong silhouette variety, mature heroic anatomy, controlled bright edge lighting and distinct costume/material groups. This is the primary visual direction for OathFront units. OathFront retains original identities, clothing, weapons and fiction.
- [Room8 Studio — Shadowveil illustrations](https://room8studio.artstation.com/projects/V2vPvn): the portfolio describes character/ability-card illustration and silhouette/brushwork refinement; its visible art uses strong central action/effect shapes. OathFront uses the production principle of one readable focal event per trick card and consistent rendering across characters and abilities.
- [Raya Castaneda — Dark Elf Rogue](https://castartstudios.artstation.com/projects/Qzxno4): the project documents final character designs, robe/armor silhouettes, card thumbnails and the final illustration. OathFront uses the workflow principle of checking silhouette and thumbnail readability before integration, rather than copying its character or a named artist's style.

## Applied direction

All 38 unit cards receive distinct original transparent figures or creatures. Humanoids use confident mature proportions, faceted armor, controlled painterly comic rendering and clear poses rather than chibi proportions. All 22 tricks use square full-scene illustrations with a single recognizable effect. The six original OathFront heroes serve as the owned material/rendering anchors.

Dawnward: ivory, jade, cyan and warm gold, with Bastion shield masses, Verdant living branches and Prism hard-light planes. Ashen Court: charcoal, crimson, amber and violet, with Furnace iron/heat forms, Hex geometric ink seals and Veil crescent/smoke forms. Silhouettes must differ by role and remain readable at card and board sizes.

Art contains no text, card frame, cost, statistics, watermark or rules. Semantic UI owns those. Exact prompts, selected source paths, hashes, alpha checks, bounded exports and review records are retained per stable card ID under `assets/source/fivefold-cards-v3`.
